#### Magical Girl + Berserker Dad Combo

### Inspiration
Cyber Magical girl and Bondrewd

### Core themes for Magical Girl

- Uses levitating energy halos as weapons. Can expand and contract at will, however must maintain close proximity with main body.

- Can stack halos on top of each other for a powerful beam attack

- Each halo can do smaller beam attacks or rapid "bullets" and will be main way for her to do bullet hells

- Halos can be arranged to boost her speed while sacrificing her ranged attacks for more closer ranged attacks, and possibly melee

- Used to be able to do summons (ie. robot/doll hacking, golem master, in exchange for all ranged and melee attacks) (under consideration)

- Gacha Power ups:
	- Increase attack speeds
	- Increase damage dealt
	- Increase number of projectiles
	- Unlock certain move sets- various forms of bullet hell
	- Increase movement speed
	- More powerful versions of beams or projectiles

### Core themes for Bondrewd Boss

- Has large cyber weapon and can have multiple forms in his later phases

- Weapon base form: claymore, almost like the one guts in berserks use
	- Slow but strong arcing attacks
	- Delayed AOE at sword impact points when it hits the ground
	- Sword Combos with openings near the end of the combo for parries or dodges or attacks
	- Parry minigame at half way point

- Weapon second form: Odachi form
	- Thinner but long large blade
	- Sweeping attacks which can be dodge checks
	- Longer range slashes, reaction checks with tight subtle indicators

- Weapon third form: Spears/Javelins
	- Thrusting attacks
	- Throwing attacks
	- Can make duplicates
	- Xayah's E to recall all thrown spears back to the user
	- Levitating weapon attacks
	- Electric fence mechanic?

- Weapon fourth form?: Dual Blades
	- Fast attacks
	- High mobility
	- Parries/dodges galore 
	- Flurry of attacks with openings at the end of the combo

- Gacha Power ups:
	- Attack speed increase along with movement speed increases
	- Energy powered attacks
	- Shockwaves that emit from ground impacts
	- Slash will emit energy waves that can behave like projectiles
	- Can unlock certain move sets like the electric fence of the spears
	- Damage increase
