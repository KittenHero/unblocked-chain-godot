# Base features

- **Omni-directional movement**  
- **Limited amount of hp**
- **Stagger bar**:
	- Bosses can be parried in certain leading to great damage to stagger bar 
	- Upon collision with wall, can lead to damage to stagger bar
	- Taking damage when the combo count is higher leads to greater damage to stagger bar

	- Still have some spin mechanic
