- gang dudes will try to surround the player and push towards boss


## Boss special attacks

When the player stay nearby for a long time:
- He chokes the player with one hand and raises them
- The player can mash buttons to get out (button indicator will show up)
- If they fail to escape, they will be slammed on the ground
- The gang dudes will kick you while you're on the ground

When the player stays far away:
- Hunch over and charge at the player
- Player can spin to escape
- If they fail, they will be slammed on the wall
- If the player escape near the wall, the boss will run into the wall, take damage, and get stunned

## The tutorial

The unblocked chain network allows users to earn rewards by using their excess compute.

*The compute bar appears on your HUD and slowly fills up*

You can boost the compute power of your node by "overclocking".
Try "overclocking" that training dummy over there.

*Training dummy materialises in the middle of the area*

Once you have reached the required amount of compute,
the network will calculate and distribute the rewards directly to you.
Keep "overclocking" to build up your unblocked chain and improve your reward.

- Dealing damage to enemies will speed up the compute bar, and raise the "chain" counter
- The chain counter will slowly tick down if there is no activity
- The chain counter will go down by a set amount when hit by an attack
- Once the compute bar fills up, it triggers a slot machine which takes 5-10 seconds to spin
- Once the slot machine is done spinning, the reward will be modified by the highest chain counter reached during the spin
- certain rewards also have a special animation/cut scene
