Game Story Script

#### Chapter One - Uber Eats and thugs and Boy meets girl


Starts off showing the main character making a uber eats delivery. He is a student, trying to make a living from uber eats. Gets bullied by the daily due to rude customers. (Customers are on the pachinko/slot machines)

Cuts between MC and to a distant chase/fight scene between a girl and a bunch of thugs.

"Get back here, thief!"

Girl has impressive parkour skills and strategically avoids thugs, but its clear that thugs have superior stats and its about to get stat checked. Girl tries to activate a phone.

Phone reads: "Activate ###? Yes or No."

Just when she is about to activate phone (or press Yes), thugs catch up and hits phone from her hand. MC just happens to be nearby as phone flies over and hits MC.

"Ouch!" Falls down from bike, sees phone nearby and picks it up, "Huh, what is this?"

Phone scans MC's face and DNA from MC's bleeding hand from the fall.

"Activation complete.. Loading parameters.."

"Huh, what?" MC confused.

Thugs shows up next to MC, girl is restrained. Thugs looks down.

"F***, its been activated."

"Move!" Girl shouts.

MC just dodges just in time as Thugs weapon goes down at where MC's head used to be.

MC gets up, scripted Boss fights starts. Escape is not an option. MC finds a bat near by and uses it as a base weapon. MC doesn't have power unlocked yet since phone artifact is not unlocked yet.

MC faces the boss with no power ups. MC is scripted to lose fight. After MC loses, his power activates. Gains a gacha weapon and beats the boss. After boss fight, girl asks MC to join her cause: to defeat an evil organisation. Girl explains that evil shadow organisation has been abusing its powerful artifacts and uses to oppress the weak and plans to take over the world. MC is reluctant since he did not ask for this, has low confidence and decides he is not fit to join the organisation.

MC's goes home. Sleeps for the day and goes to work in the next morning.

### Chapter Two - Karen Encounter

Goes visits the usual Karen customer. Distract by yesterday events, he has forgot the Karen's favourite sauce for her fried chicken. Karen begins a her rant and then it escalates as it turns out the Karen has been possessed and turned even more evil due to an implanted chip by the evil company (evil company is a famous cybernetics company and has backdoor access to all their customers implanted chips). Turns out the evil company has been keeping an eye on him and due to the power's influence his chip has not been possessed. Evil Karen prepares a hidden attack but gets interrupted by girl who arrives on time. Fight against evil Karen ensures.

After fight with Karen, magical girl boss arrives, with the signal that Karen gave off she was dispatched to the sight as she is one of the fastest in the evil organisation. She beats MC easily. As she is about to finish MC off, Girl saves MC once more time knocking down the magical girl and flings MC to the side and where he runs and hides. Magical girl, seeing that she has lost MC, captures Girl and go back to her hideout. Girl leaves behind her phone which includes her GPS location and her power boosts believing him to be their saviour. MC is moved by her actions and with the recent attack of Karen made the evil organisation threat very real to him. He vows to save her and visits the enemy HQ the next day.

### Chapter Three - Final boss

MC meets up with the magical girl boss. Fights magical girl phase one. Bondrewd inspo boss joins for phase two, does some evil monologue and joins the fight paired with the magical girl. After win against the boss duo, MC makes cool victory monologue and eradicates boss. MC frees Girl and destroys HQ's main core which corrupts chip implants. MC saves the day and also the magical girl as it seems like she was under the control of bondrewd boss. Potential for future games as the base MC destroyed is just one of the many subsidiaries of the organisation.
