# Karen Boss Design Document

## Boss Overview

Karen, UberEats delivery guy forgot her delivery. 
She hangry.
## Core Mechanics

## Combat

### Enemy Moveset Phase 1

- **Granny Scooter**:
	- Entity blows whistle, brief window to calibrate direction
	- Puts on cybernetic helmet
	- Speeds in a straight line direction towards player
	- If collide into wall, knocked down and increase stagger bar
	- Deals X damage
	- Can be parried
- **Verbal Diarrhea**:
	- Throws circle shaped cloud full of symbols 
	- Deals X damage
	- Can use up to 3-5 in succession 
	- Leaves red indicator in place of impact
	- Cannot be parried
- **Look what big teeth you have**:
	- Chomp attack
	- Can be parried
	- Fast
	- Deals X damage
- **Oops dropped my slap cane**:
	- Flying walking canes
	- Can be parried
	- Bullet hell patterns
	- Deals X damage

### Enemy Moveset Phase 2

- **Granny Scooter**:
	- Scooter smoking
	- Faster travel
- **Verbal Diarrhea**:
	- Cloud split into 4 directions upon impact
- **Look what big teeth you have**:
	- Makes 2 chomp attacks in quick succession
- **Oops dropped my slap cane**:
	- Faster projectile speed
### Win Condition

Deliver food-slap! Take hit points to 0
